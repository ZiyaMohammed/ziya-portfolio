I care about the people on the other side of the screen, and about who gets to build what's on it.

I'm the Experience Design Manager at Ashoka Changemakers. I set the experience strategy for social change programmes and platforms, and run research and impact evaluation. I write the documentation and SOPs that keep the work consistent, and design the experience for the people on the team as well as the people we serve. Everything we ship has to work for diverse and underserved communities, and be accessible.

Before that I spent five years as Design Lead at She Code Africa, owning its creative direction and every design resource it put out: campaign flyers and resources, sponsorship and partnership decks, pitch decks, presentation slides and templates. I led the creative team for the annual Summit, oversaw the website and launched a bi-weekly design talk show. I've also designed for WriteTech Hub, Accessibility Nigeria, where I led design resources, DalO Systems, Outsource Global, Blue Sapphire and DevCareer.

Alongside that, I'm building two things of my own. Muse & Bloom shares the stories of Muslim women across Africa building careers and lives on their own terms, through a podcast, open letters and an archive of women doing remarkable things. Muse Studios is a design studio for organisations working on social change, and a way to create paid work for underrepresented designers.

I also give my time to the communities that helped me grow. I'm an Ambassador for Women Techmakers Lokoja, a design lead at GDG Lokoja and Accessibility Nigeria, and a design mentor for 3MTT.
